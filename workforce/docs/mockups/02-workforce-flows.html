Workforce — the flows, for the owner's read. What happens between the screens: seven paths end to end, each one drawn with the branch that goes wrong beside the one that goes right. The gold page shows what a screen looks like; this page shows what the platform does when somebody presses the button — which transaction, which event, which check, and what is refused rather than warned. Every step names the ruling behind it. Same window language as the gold (ADR 0106); nothing here is a new decision.
How to read a step. A plain box is something a person does or sees. tinted is an event the application publishes. amber is a duty or time fact. A dashed box is where a path stops. REFUSED and WARNED are the two different things this application does when something is wrong, and §8 shows every one of them in one place — they are not interchangeable, and WF-Q16 is the rule that separates them.

Why not a Kernel RPC that writes the tuple?A gRPC call cannot join the caller's transaction. A crash between the two would keep the posting and lose its authorization — silently, and in the safe-looking direction. So the event is written with the row and relayed afterwards.
Before any posting existsDepartment membership is empty by design (ADR 0116 §6) — not broken, not pending a migration. There is no interim mechanism to replace, which is why this flow is slice 1 and everything else waits.

Why the accept step exists at allIt costs one state and buys the thing that matters: a manager's approval must never commit somebody who did not agree. Without it, approving a swap volunteers a person's Saturday for them.
Why this is not the manager's ⇄ Swap buttonThat one is an action — pick two people, exchange their week, done, consent-free, because the manager is the authority. This one is an object with a state. They look identical on the rota and are not the same thing.
REFUSED · one of the two shifts no longer existsA swap needs both sides. If the other shift was deleted or reassigned while the proposal sat waiting, approval is refused rather than half-applied — WF-Q16.
Not drawn: the staff member proposing it themselvesWorkforce is a manager and HR application — "this is not access by every staff" — and the staff app is next version. So in v1 every proposal arrives on behalf, and entered_by is what keeps that honest.

WARNED · the balance is exhaustedThe request is still made, and can still be approved. The manager sees −1 of 15 and decides — hotels override reality daily. A balance may go negative, and the screens are drawn against that (WF-Q5).
Cancelled after approvalThe debit is credited back. A ledger with a debit and no matching credit turns a cancellation into a silent forfeit.
A gap is a mark, not a calculationThe vacated cell reads as needing a cover. It is deliberately not a computed staffing shortfall — that needs a required-headcount model, which is the doorway to coverage rules and shift-bidding, and nothing in eight subjects asked for one.
Comp-off does not arrive hereHolidays worked are counted by the month-end numbers; HR grants the credit by hand in v1. Automatic credit waits for device attendance at least — WF-Q13.

Late, and kept"Keep late information" — so lateness is a first-class fact. But there is no late-minutes column: 07:00 and 07:20 are the facts, "late 20 min" is arithmetic over them, and a stored number can disagree with the two times beside it. Store the boundary facts, compute the judgment.
AbsentPosted, no record. Shown against the rota; nothing is inferred about why.
WARNED · present, and not rosteredAttendance contradicting the rota. Both facts are kept and the discrepancy is shown — never silently reconciled into one, because either of them may be the true one and the system does not know which.
The device is not built hereA fingerprint or face terminal is a connector: it installs into the Integration Hub, declares its own authentication, and the Hub owns dedupe — so a terminal that was offline overnight replays a batch in the morning and the same punch does not become two.

REFUSED · two duties overlapping"Who is MOD now" with two answers is a corrupt record, not a judgment call. Assigning over an existing duty replaces it behind a named confirm — which is a different thing from allowing both.
No is_current_mod flagNo stored "current", no nightly job moving a marker. Every one of those is a value that can be wrong while the data beside it is right — the same reason the platform derives the current business date instead of storing it.
A gap is drawn, not hiddenOne duty ends at 08:00, the next begins at 20:00, and nobody holds it in between. The register shows dashed "no MOD" — a blank cell could mean nobody or not entered yet, and those are different.
The duty grants nothingMOD is a duty assignment, not an authorization role — no tuples in v1. The person keeps their own posting: security stays security. Any future elevation needs its own authorization ADR, and duty.assigned is the hook it would use.

Why the Context answer matters more than the screenIf "who can do X" returned a bare list, the first consumer to care about expiry would re-implement the rule, the second would implement it differently, and "we didn't know" becomes true again one application away.

8 · Refused, or warned — one rule, applied once, so that no screen has to decide it again

WF-Q16: the platform refuses the physically impossible or self-contradicting, and warns on a judgment. That is why a double-booked room is refused loudly while an exhausted leave balance is merely warned — they were never in tension, they sit on opposite sides of one line. Every guard in this application, in one place:
Refused · the record would contradict itself
Two MOD duties overlapping"Who is MOD now" would have two answers. Assigning over an existing duty replaces it behind a named confirm.
One person, two overlapping shiftsA record saying somebody worked two places at once.
A swap whose two shifts do not both existApproval would half-apply, leaving one cell changed and one not.
Warned, and named · a person decides
An exhausted leave balanceShown as −1 of 15. Hotels override reality daily — WF-Q5.
Planned hours over the OT thresholdAt planning time, on planned hours — WF-Q14.
An expired certification on assignmentNamed, never blocking — the hotel's judgment.
Attendance contradicting the rotaBoth facts kept, the difference shown — WF-Q10.
A leave-vacated slot with no coverA mark on the cell, not a computed shortfall.
The distinction is not severity, and that is the part worth keeping. An expired fire-warden card may matter far more to a hotel than a double-booked room — and it is still only warned, because a person with a lapsed certificate can work the shift and the question is whether they should. A room cannot hold two stays; a person cannot be in two places; "who is MOD now" cannot have two answers. The line is between what is impossible and what is a decision — never between what is important and what is not.
What is deliberately not drawn here. No mobile paths — the staff app is next version, and these flows publish the events it will subscribe to, which is the whole of what v1 owes it. No device-attendance configuration: a terminal arrives as a connector, configured in the Integration Hub. No payroll path beyond the export — the numbers leave this application as a file and what happens to them is Finance's or the hotel's. And no permission-elevation path for the MOD, because WF-Q1 ruled the duty carries none; the event is published so that a future ADR has something to hang an elevation from, and nothing more.
