Workforce — the gold design, for the owner's approval. Who works here, as what, where, and when. Five sections, one primary action per screen, and three marks the whole app is built from: a shift chip in three tints, a struck day for leave, and the MOD ribbon for the Manager on Duty — a duty for the day, never a change of role. Frames are the desktop window at 1220 wide; the rail, sheet, dialog and confirmation rule are the platform's (ADR 0106), unchanged.

The owner's exact scenario. Rahul Nair is Security. On Wednesday and Saturday he is also MOD. Nothing about his posting moves; the register says who holds the duty and the calendars show it.
