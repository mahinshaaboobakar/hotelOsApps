Four things the build does that nobody has decided

Each was found by measuring the build against its frames, and each was reported in prose and never ruled. They are drawn here because a drawing moves a decision that an explanation does not. They are four separate decisions — deciding one says nothing about the others, and any of them may be answered with something not drawn.

Every option below is drawn in the product's own pieces, on the published colours. as built marks what a person meets today. measured marks a fact I checked in the code rather than recalled. I argue against marks the arm I would not choose, with its reason — stated so the case against each is in front of you, not only the case for.
