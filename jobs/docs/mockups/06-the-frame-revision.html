Mockups 01-the-jobs-screens.html and 02-the-jobs-settings.html were locked by you on 2026-09-04. They stand at b346957. A · The palette: 13 names removed, 1 visible change

On 2026-09-05 the frames' colours were moved onto the published tokens, but the page's own names were kept as aliases, e.g. --indigo: var(--color-brand). Every alias already resolves to a published token or to a mix of them, so removing the aliases changes nothing you can see. What changes is that the drawing now names only what a module can actually use (§1: "a mock that invents --indigo is teaching a vocabulary that cannot reach a running module").

B · The dates, drawn in en-GB and labelled that way

Page 64 §11: "A frame states a date's shape … and never its characters. Where a frame must show something, it shows the locale it was drawn in, labelled as such." The frames say only that Jobs renders from the property's locale. They then draw 02 Sep 14:10, which isn't what any locale's formatter produces. en-GB, for example, writes September as Sept.

Every drawn date was run through the SDK formatter the platform uses for its kind, with locale en-GB and timezone Asia/Qatar. Nothing here was retyped by hand. This line goes into both frames' headers:

Dates on this page are drawn in en-GB for a property in Asia/Qatar. This is one locale's output. A property sees its own (page 64 §11).

Unchanged: 17 hours of day, 1 time and 9 durations

Hours of day (00:00, 06:00, 07:00, 08:00, 14:00, 22:00, 23:00) go through formatClock. In en-GB it writes the same characters the frames already draw. en-US would write 07:00 AM, so the locale line above covers these too. One time-only instant (P1 raised 13:31) goes through formatInstant · time and is also unchanged in en-GB. Durations (00:10:00, 00:23:41, 00:31:12) go through formatDuration, which doesn't depend on the locale.

C · Two corrections to the approved frames

correction Four dates carry 2025's weekday. Frame 6 (Scheduled) draws Wed 3 Sep, Mon 15 Sep and Fri 19 Sep, and two cells draw a stay that departs Thu 4 Sep. correction The header prose says the numbers are "the numbers those screens would show on a Tuesday afternoon." 2 September 2026 is a Wednesday. The word becomes Wednesday.

D1 · A day with its weekday and month: no platform style draws it

Seven cells draw a calendar day as weekday, day and month: Wed 3 Sep. Four are in Scheduled's first column, two are a stay's departure, and one is the Board strip's day. formatDay has four styles and none of them gives that shape. weekday-day drops the month and day-month drops the weekday. As drawn, those cells describe something the build can't render in any locale. This is a design choice and it's yours (ARCH-Q10). Each option is drawn below with its weekday corrected.
