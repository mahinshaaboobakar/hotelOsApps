F · flows

  • Guest raises from the room. Guest app (stay link) or room QR → job RAISED with raised_via, stay_id → flow sets priority from PMS occupancy → AUTO assigns from Workforce's on-shift list → frame 2 → resolve (frame 4) → auto-close → guest rates (S6, S10).
  • Concern escalates. Sweep every 60 s → AT_RISK at the policy's percentage → nudge assignee → BREACHED → accountable climbs the ladder → nudge the subscriber → frame 5 and the widget change; nothing on the job row changes except what the sweep derives (S5).
  • Shift starts. Workforce publishes shift.started → Jobs marks the department present → clocks that paused outside service hours resume; nothing polls Workforce (S7).
  • PPM arrives. Engineering's plan fires on Temporal Cron → maintenance.ppm.due → SCHEDULED parent with child steps → RAISED at 00:00 of the day (frame 6).
  • Hold with a date. Put on hold with hold_reason + hold_until → clock stopped → reminder before the date to the policy's role → resume or reassign (S9 D2).

Deliberately not drawn

  • The guest app's raise and rating screens — the guest-app round's, reached by stay link or QR (S6, S10 D2). Jobs renders neither.
  • WhatsApp intake — a channel, not a screen; it lands as raised_via WHATSAPP.
  • A notifications settings page — none exists (S9 D10).
  • A permission editor — grants are Identity's (S8 D1, AUTHZ-Q25); Jobs only displays what was granted.
