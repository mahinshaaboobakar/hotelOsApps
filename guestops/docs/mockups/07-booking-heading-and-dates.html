BA stay's dates — two days, or the shorter range?

Everywhere a stay's span is drawn: the lists, a booking's stays, and the cancellation dialog.

The drawings used the short form and the build now uses the long one. A range like 3 – 7 September puts the month at the end because that is how English is written here; a property reading American English writes the same two days Sep 3 – 7. The service used to write the short form itself, in one language, so it moved to the screen as two plain days. The short form is available correctly — the browser knows each language's own way of joining a range — and the strings below are what it actually produces.
