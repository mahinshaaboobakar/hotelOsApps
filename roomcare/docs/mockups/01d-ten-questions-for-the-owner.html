Ten questions about Room Care's screens

Each is drawn both ways in the product's own pieces, and followed to what a person sees next rather than stopped at the choice. Six are words on a screen; four are how a screen behaves. Every option here works — none is a defect — and I have marked the one I would choose and said why, so you can disagree with a reason rather than with a preference.

Nothing waits on these: Room Care 0.1.5 is built, verified and staged. These are the choices I did not want to make on your behalf.

  1. Saying “PMS” on a screen
  2. Naming the other applications
  3. Two columns that are always empty
  4. The Photo button that does nothing yet
  5. Two notes that explain the system
  6. States written in capitals

—Three more, about the drawings rather than the screens

These came out of comparing the approved frames with the built screens, and they are differences between the two rather than choices about behaviour. They need a word from you only if you disagree with what was built: a blocked room is dimmed two different ways on the wall — “off the day” and “out of order” — and the Suite row on Services is drawn selected, and slightly smaller than the table around it. Say if you want either redrawn; otherwise the build stands and the frames follow it.

What these are not. None of the ten is a fault and none blocks the install. The screens work as drawn today. Each is a place where two reasonable answers exist, and the one in the build is mine rather than yours.
