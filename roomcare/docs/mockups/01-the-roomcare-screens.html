F · deliberately not drawn

  • A roster, a shift, an attendance record, a capacity number — Workforce's. The proposal reads them through Context and draws only who is here.
  • A request form — "clean now", water, towels, a spill — Jobs'. Room Care shows job.closed in the room's day and nothing else (S5 c8).
  • A guest-preference form — GuestOps's; the wish reaches the board by event, or until then by roomcare.amend on frame 4.
  • A catalogue, a price, a stock, a cart — Inventory's (RC-Q2). The attendant's restock line is absent until it is installed.
  • A checklist or an inspection screen — the inspection application's (RC-Q1(6)).
  • An out-of-order control — the state's owner's; Room Care requests a block by event.
  • A public-area editor — Master Data's location tree already holds lobby, corridor, restaurant, pool, terrace, back of house (masterdata/v1/dto.proto:207–219); Room Care draws their schedules in Setup › Areas.
  • A weekday calendar of cleaning kinds, a preferred attendant, a per-stay cadence, a long-stay kind — ruled out (hotels only; the reference's apartment shape).
  • A multi-day assignment — Jobs' (JOBS-Q2).
  • Any chat, SMS or WhatsApp — the platform's notifications.
  • A "why did I get 19 rooms" explainer — the proposal is deterministic by the property's strategy and the row's decision record answers it; HosPilot reads that record aloud (S7).
